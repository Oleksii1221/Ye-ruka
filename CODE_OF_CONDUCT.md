# Code of Conduct

Ye-Ruka contributors are expected to communicate professionally, assume good intent, and make room for people with different levels of hardware and software experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of private information, and deliberately unsafe advice are not accepted. Technical disagreement is welcome when it remains specific, evidence-based, and respectful.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when this policy is violated. Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through GitHub.
